Age filters are provisional content suggestions, based on each world’s theme and movement. They are not clinical approval or a validated age rating.

For ages 10–12, use adult supervision and an eligible parent-managed headset account. Follow the headset’s age rules, local requirements and your care team’s advice. This catalogue does not recommend headset use below age 10.
